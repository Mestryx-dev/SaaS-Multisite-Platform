## Positioning

- Multi-brand **CMS** (core) with room for commerce, modules, later Web3  
- Dogfood first: Mestryx runs a real site + business on Auralis  
- Brand-first name (not descriptive “Sites/Hub”)

## Tone of voice

- Precise, confident, international  
- FR for Mestryx-facing marketing when needed; **EN mandatory** for product UI/docs public; more locales later  
- Code, APIs, system strings: **English only**
